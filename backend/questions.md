# Open Decisions (do not block scaffold)

1. Official domains: production FE/admin/API/CDN; use example placeholders until provided.
2. Official per-branch phone/Zalo/email and social channel URLs; never invent.
3. Product source catalog and manufacturer authorization, warranty facts/certification claims.
4. Branch permissions: one manager per branch or cross-branch shared agents?
5. Media budget: cloud storage provider, video hosting/CDN, upload size caps.
6. AI provider/model budget, privacy constraints, approval delegation; deterministic fallback mandatory.
7. Whether prices are publicly displayed; default quote-only (no cart/order/payment).
8. Languages after Vietnamese; DB localization-ready, MVP `vi-VN`.
9. Legal privacy policy version, retention limits and notification preferences.
10. Confirmation of desktop/mobile preferred breakpoints and target browser matrix.

Defaults in implementation plan remain valid and are reversible without hardcoding business data.
